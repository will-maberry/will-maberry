# Hi, I'm Will Maberry 👋

Computational epidemiology researcher working at the intersection of infectious disease surveillance, outbreak forecasting, and population medicine.

---

### About

I am a **Graduate Research Assistant** in the Department of Pathobiology and Population Medicine at Mississippi State University's College of Veterinary Medicine, pursuing an **M.S. in Veterinary and Biomedical Sciences** (Population Medicine) funded by a USDA ARS NACA fellowship. 

My current work is focused on H5N1 highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI):
* **Biosecurity case-control study:** My master's thesis focuses on farm-level biosecurity practices at poultry operations in the Midwestern United States, assessing outbreak risk and drivers.
* **Early-warning detection:** Temporally constrained, leakage-safe machine learning (ML) modeling for look-ahead county-level risk forecasting.
* **Methods & tools:** Classical epidemiologic study design, spatiotemporal modeling, rare-event classification, R, Python, Quarto, Jupyter Notebooks, and LaTeX.
